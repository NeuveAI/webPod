## Behavior and correctness

- Tap/click opaque placed artwork selects without lifting/writing. A thin on-object selection frame with clear handles animates into place. No large rectangular form, property tabs, title block, bottom dock or automatic camera reframe on selection.
- Corner grips resize with aspect ratio preserved; edge/outside grips rotate with clear affordance. Use actual projected visible-art corners and plane mapping, including model pose/perspective. Angle unwrap across±180 must not jump; rotate/size stay in existing safe bounds without silently moving the center. Small stickers still have distinct reachable touch targets; do not hide overlap behind44px CSS claims.
- Body drag retains existing physical lift/move/press and wear. Handles own their pointer without shell flick or body pickup. Every direct adjustment follows pointer immediately; springs belong to HUD enter, release and exit, not delayed cursor chasing. Re-grab/interrupt/cancel remain responsive.
- Completed gesture commits one guarded revisioned edit. Preview remains separate from inventory. Escape/capture cancellation reverts the active gesture without write. Pending save is indicated locally and subtly; failure restores authority with compact retry/undo recovery, and409 requires deliberate re-edit. Stale sent responses cannot revive selection or apply to another print. A single Undo restores the previous completed customization through a guarded write.
- A compact icon group exposes wear, return and undo/dismiss. Wear may reveal one small control near its icon. Names and hints are accessible labels/tooltips on focus/hover, not permanent explanatory text. Keyboard selection/rotation/resize/wear and returning remain operable through handles or small progressive precise controls; no default settings panel.
- Existing owned wear survives return/re-stick/reload. Routine ready/stuck/partial notices remain visually absent. Actionable errors stay contextual. Expired/logged-out sessions clear HUD and pending gesture state. Selection switch, flip, resize, visibility loss and component teardown cancel owned motion/listeners.
