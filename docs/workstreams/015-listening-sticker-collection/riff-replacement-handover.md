## Creative direction and assessment

The shared brief required the motif to determine the silhouette, expressive lettering integrated into a banner or illustrated structure, worn print beneath a continuous pale edge, and meaningful negative space. Night Shift and No Skip established the craft reference; the rejected generic black assembly was an explicit anti-reference. No native-path collage was used for these illustrations.

Heavy Rotation combines an oblique LP, thick chain and torn typography banner. Its distinct silhouette and title/record hierarchy communicate repeated heavy listening most clearly, including at pocket scale. Brand recommends this direction for selection.

Riff Ritual combines a guitar headstock and amplifier with expressive RIFF lettering and a wrapping RITUAL ribbon. It evokes an instrument scene more directly, with more grille/cable detail and a busier silhouette. It remains legible at the pocket proof size.
