## Shared proposed recipe

Warm ivory opaque vinyl (`#F3EBD7`), visually thin and flexible, with a consistent approximately 0.7–1 mm die-cut perimeter at final size. Spot-colour print character includes fine pores, occasional halftone, and slight registration imperfection. Wear belongs to the ink under an intact satin laminate. Keep silhouettes intact and major artwork clear at 18–35 mm scale.

Carbon `#191A19`, bone `#DBD8C6`, hot pink `#FD3D8D`, cyan `#1AD9E7`, lime `#D8EC44`, orange `#FF7135`. Palettes can diverge; stock, cutline, grain density and satin reflection stay related. A tiny optional silver detail may appear, never a full-surface hologram or domed resin badge. These are visual proposals, not vendor-ready manufacturing specifications.

Material images are explicitly AI-generated imagined production proofs, not archival photographs. They show abstract print scraps and edge details, deliberately no finished pack artwork before brand handoff.

## Grounding

- [Sticker Mule custom stickers](https://www.stickermule.com/custom-stickers): vinyl with protective laminate and shape-following die cuts support the physical construction direction.
- [StickerGiant die cuts](https://support.stickergiant.com/hc/en-us/articles/38930337230484-What-Are-Die-Cut-Stickers): demonstrates thin laminated sticker construction; its stated stock is polypropylene, not vinyl. Used only as finish/thickness context, not proof of our proposed vinyl stock.
- [StickerGiant clear finishes](https://support.stickergiant.com/hc/en-us/articles/235956948-What-Are-Clear-Stickers): matte finish describes low-glare surface character. Our satin choice is an artistic intermediate, not a claim about that vendor's offering.
