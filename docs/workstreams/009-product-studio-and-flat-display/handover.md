# Product studio and flat display

The procedural scene now uses three real area lights: elevated key, broad lower-left fill, and rear strip rim, with controlled studio reflections and AgX for physical highlights. This is an interpretation informed by primary product-photography sources in research.md, not a proprietary Apple recipe. Astra implemented; a separate investigator diagnosed the LCD and captured final evidence; the lead supervised research and whole-device appearance; an independent reviewer verified implementation and checks.

The apparent screen warp was the body's remaining inner bevel occluding a flat LCD. Correct contour selection removes that intrusion while preserving the outer shell crown. Black and white front captures have a straight upper active edge at all seven sampled positions. Quarter views remain clean; extreme sidewall occlusion of a recessed screen is explicitly modeled in regression tests. Underlying LCD colors bypass AgX and were pixel-identical in the investigator's isolated comparison; physical glass contributes a small separate brightness change.

Lead inspected final front/quarter LCD bezels, black/white whole-device candidates, all five constant-environment light passes, and rear reflection views. The back retains dark-to-silver gradients and controlled edge highlights. At the rotated quarter pose, the broad front reflection is largely the world-fixed rim; the lower-left fill gently raises the wheel. Lamp roles naturally change relative to the turning product. The bounded 30-view rear sweep's central ROI reports no strict or near-white saturation; this excludes edge highlights and does not claim every possible angle or calibrated photometry.

Validation: independent 347 unit/integration tests, device/composite/web TypeScript, scoped lint, and eight relevant orientation/Settings browser tests pass. Only the documented pre-existing native text-selection browser expectation was excluded, because it conflicts with prior user-select:none behavior. Reintroducing the original bevel selection defect into an isolated copied module makes the border regression fail. No production mutation was used for evidence capture.
