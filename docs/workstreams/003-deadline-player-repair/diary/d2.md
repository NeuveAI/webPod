# D2 diary — compact period iPod/Aqua presentation

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Implementer:** `/root/d2_aqua_visual`
**Status:** implementation and evidence complete; independent review pending

## Context and critique

This is the 272×204 LCD of a fifth-generation-inspired iPod music player. The owner is preparing a deadline demo, so selection clarity, fast visual parsing, and believable period character matter more than adding decoration.

The supplied current-state captures had three dominant problems:

1. Rows, selected rows, titles, counts, and secondary metadata were all rendered with nearly the same heavy typographic voice. At device scale this made the LCD feel inflated and left no hierarchy.
2. The saturated cyan selected row and saturated cyan scroll thumb competed as two progress-like bars. Selection was clear, but the material read as generic glossy cyan rather than restrained period Aqua.
3. The 7px rail occupied too much of a 168px split list pane and could overlap end-of-row content. The preview artwork also filled almost all of its pane, leaving the selected entity and instruction visually secondary.

## Design decisions

- Preserved the canonical 21px title bar, 183px list viewport, 8-row window, and one shared row primitive. Geometry and navigation were already correct.
- Kept all authored panel text at 11px or larger, but changed rows from semibold/bold selection to a constant medium weight. Secondary metadata and tabular cells use regular weight. Because selection no longer changes font metrics, moving the highlight cannot shift columns or truncation.
- Shifted the selection palette from bright teal to steel blue. Two restrained opaque gradients and the structural 1px rim preserve the period gel/glass vocabulary without making the row look like a billboard.
- Reduced the rail from 7px to 5px, retained stationary 1px ribs, and kept the thumb as a separate layered object. Overflow rows reserve 10px at the inline end so the rail never covers content; lists that fit still render no rail.
- Reduced only menu-preview artwork to 74px. Now Playing's established 88px artwork geometry remains untouched.
- Added explicit `prefers-contrast: more` hierarchy tokens and forced-colour rail construction. Existing reduced-motion and reduced-transparency branches remain intact.

The workstream's period-Aqua direction intentionally overrides the generic design guardrail against gradients. Every retained gradient describes a material boundary (title chrome, selection glass, recessed trough, gel thumb), not decoration.

## Quality facets

| Facet | Before | After | Why |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Legible | 2/5 | 4/5 | Title, primary label, metadata, and counts no longer share one heavy voice. |
| Restrained | 1/5 | 4/5 | Steel-blue selection and a 5px rail stop competing with the music data. |
| Period-authentic | 2/5 | 4/5 | Crisp rim, opaque gel depth, stationary ribs, and compact Helvetica hierarchy read as purposeful Aqua. |
| Stable | 4/5 | 5/5 | All row geometry is retained and selection uses identical font metrics. |
| Adaptable | 3/5 | 4/5 | Both colourways and contrast/forced-colour modes now have explicit selection and rail tokens. |

Owner visual approval remains the authority for the subjective period-authenticity score.
