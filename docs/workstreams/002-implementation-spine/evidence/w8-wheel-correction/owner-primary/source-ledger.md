iPod 5G on 2026-09-01. They were inspected individually at their original
4032×3024 resolution before any subsequent geometry or material edit. Their
filenames are preserved here verbatim. Derived contact and comparison boards
contain no embedded source metadata; this ledger is the provenance record.

These owner photographs supersede marketplace, refurbished-product and image-
search material for the white colorway. Apple archival imagery, iFixit repair
photography and contemporary reviews remain independent geometry cross-checks.

## Color-management rule

Raw pixels are not material swatches. The room light is warm, the glossy shell
contains nearby-display spill, and phone processing/white balance are unknown.
The model therefore preserves only relationships repeated across the set:

- the faceplate is a warm white/ivory glossy polycarbonate;
- the wheel is a cooler, darker light grey with a more matte response;
- the Select is a separate cream/white part closer to the faceplate than to the
  wheel, but with its own roughness and highlight;
- `MENU` and all three transport legends are light/white on this OEM unit,
  never the previous medium dark grey;
- neither white nor black is derived by inverting the other colorway.

## Multi-view normalized constraints

Perspective-bounded measurements from `IMG_2239`, `IMG_2240`, `IMG_2248` and
`IMG_2249` were compared with the orthographic Apple archive rather than used
alone. They support these ranges:

- wheel diameter: approximately `61–64%` of body width, consistent with the
  Apple-derived `235 / 377 = 62.33%` model ratio;
- Select diameter: approximately `21–23%` of body width, consistent with the
  Apple-derived `84 / 377 = 22.28%` model ratio;
- wheel center: approximately `70–71%` of body height from the top, consistent
  with the model's `390 / 552 = 70.65%` placement;
- active-display top: approximately `4.5–5.5%` of body height after allowing
  for perspective and trim, bounding rather than replacing the model's `4.35%`;
- visible wheel seam: hairline, under roughly `1%` of wheel diameter;
- visible Select seam: hairline, approximately `1–2%` of Select diameter;
- axial ordering in `IMG_2242`: faceplate proud of wheel, wheel proud of
  Select, with both offsets smaller than their radial seams.

The owner set independently confirms the current body, screen, wheel and Select
ratios. This correction does not rescale those parts; it corrects white material
relationships and tightens the already bounded near-coplanar assembly.

## Remaining uncertainty / requested missing shot

The nine angles establish relative ordering but do not calibrate absolute
faceplate→wheel or wheel→Select depth in millimetres. To claim either as an OEM
dimension, the missing source is a stationary, near-orthographic macro edge
photograph containing faceplate, wheel edge and Select edge together, with a
ruler or feeler gauge in the same focal plane. Until then, model depth values are
explicitly visual bounds inside the observed hairline-seam envelope.
