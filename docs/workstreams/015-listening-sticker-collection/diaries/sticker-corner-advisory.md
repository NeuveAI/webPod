## Diagnosis that changes the next experiment

The exact adjacent-facet Fermi result is not a valid starting sheet: the reported 32-cell minimum stretch is .00194, with 12 of 479 wholly flat-rear triangles reversed near (138,266,-28). The separate unsupported front/hold crossings cannot explain that supported-rear failure. This establishes failure of that embedding, not impossibility of physical wrapping. Do not lift its vertices along normals and call the result a wrinkle: that retains the lost material metric.

A local ridge is also not a free correction variable. A tent cross-section of rest width L and endpoint distance d has height sqrt(L²-d²)/2 when its two equal sides are straight. Keeping endpoint positions while adding arbitrary height changes L. Sweeping that cross-section along a curved trajectory adds further longitudinal/shear constraints. The formula is a useful necessary local check, not a 3D corner construction. In particular the earlier two-unit outward allowance is not justified by geometry alone.

An uncut interior cone/crease vertex has a material angle budget of 2π. Removing a wedge, compressing its UV angle, or allowing fan panels to miss one another silently changes that budget. A crease can instead reach a physical sheet boundary, where there is no closed interior vertex cycle. A terminating wrinkle can be physical, but its core must be modeled and its strain measured; a zero-area decorative apex is not that model.

Primary research supports the qualitative choice of localized delamination/folding, not a numerical material model for this vinyl. The 2023 PNAS study [Delamination from an adhesive sphere](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10041104/) investigates curvature-induced delamination and buckling. Its spherical specimens do not establish a permissible lift or strain for this device. [Curved Crease Origami](https://people.csail.mit.edu/edemaine/papers/CurvedCrease_AAG2008/paper.pdf) distinguishes crease geometry from physical equilibrium and documents that superficially similar crease patterns need not fold compatibly. My boundary-fan construction below is a geometric proposal, not an algorithm claimed from either paper.

## One small, falsifiable construction: boundary-apex hinge fan with a compliant seam collar

Test only the existing maximum C03, width115.5, height228.32285, center (157,268,3.1122703), angle0. Do not begin 45/137-degree poses until this one has a complete passing mesh. Keep its original planar material coordinates, alpha and print. Use the production support/collider including independently verified real-gap bridges. Missing support is a failed/incomplete test, never omitted triangles.

The proposed family is deliberately restricted: one corner-local fan of 24 rigid material triangles sharing an apex on the physical outer sheet boundary, joined to the valid direct-strip regions through a declared 12-unit-wide compliant collar. Choose the fan's boundary outlet along the corner's outward bisector in material coordinates; intersect that ray with the full material rectangle, not an alpha hole. The fan must include the saved-center corner neighborhood and reach that outlet. Its remaining boundary is the material seam with supported strips. Record this polygon and all material coordinates before solving. It must be star-shaped about the chosen apex; otherwise this particular family fails at construction and is not replaced with arbitrary overlapping fans. Twelve units is an experimental localization bound, not a vinyl material constant.

Freeze the following explicit material polygon for this angle-zero fixture, with material axes oriented as the fixture's original modelXY placement: full rectangle [-57.75,57.75] × [-114.161425,114.161425]; patch P=[-18,57.75] × [-18,114.161425]. The -18 offsets are the source tangency coordinates139/250 minus saved modelXY157/268. This is a deterministic rest-layout choice only, not a claim that nominal projected XY forms a physical surface chart. Its outward 45-degree bisector first meets the physical rectangle at outlet q=(57.75,57.75). Use the 12-unit L-shaped collar inside P along its u=-18 and v=-18 boundaries, rows at offsets0/6/12, sharing the12×12 corner junction. The rigid fan occupies [-6,57.75] × [-6,114.161425], contains the material center, and shares outlet q on its boundary. Fan its perimeter to q with 24 nondegenerate panels, retaining all rectangle corners as perimeter vertices. The left collar edge attaches to the top developed region, the bottom edge to the right developed region; their outside lower-left transition shares the rear region. Both phases derive from the common root constraints, with the collar supplying only its declared compliance. If those source-backed developments do not support these exact material boundaries compatibly, stop before contact search. Do not resize or move the seam to get a pass. This may reject the family; it avoids choosing a patch from the failed Fermi embedding.

This is a restricted-kinematic feasibility experiment, not a guarantee that a single fan can cover the actual doubly curved region. A curved strip attachment is a serious compatibility risk. Before a collision search, establish whether its mismatch can fit the compliant collar. For every original collar material edge of length l, its endpoint distance must lie in the declared stretch interval times l; a violated upper bound is an immediate necessary-condition rejection. Check triangle principal stretches as well, because edge bounds alone do not exclude shear. Do not spend time optimizing contact for an already incompatible seam. The lead reports the accepted maximum strip's measured .956–1.153 stretch envelope; use that as this experiment's comparison envelope, not a claim about physical vinyl yield. Apply it to the full collar topology and report occupied-print values separately.

For each fan triangle, preserve the three original planar side lengths. Build its pose by forward rigid hinge rotations from one root triangle: rotate each next triangle about their shared material ray. This gives C0 continuity and unit singular values within each rendered triangle by construction, with no whole-sheet PBD and no UV resampling. Render those same planar panels, permitting normal discontinuities only on the named physical creases. A boundary apex avoids inventing an interior angular deficit. It does not by itself guarantee attachment compatibility, shell clearance, or absence of self-overlap.

Fix the root frame using the saved center and its original local orientation; if the center lies within a root triangle, constrain its barycentric point exactly. There are at most 23 hinge-angle unknowns, plus one coherent longitudinal phase per adjoining translational strip. These phase variables allow contact to slide while the center stays fixed; they must not alter stored placement coordinates or independently distort rows. Add two collar rings at six-unit spacing, with at most 48 free three-dimensional vertices; the fan and strip boundary vertices are shared. The collar is an explicit elastic/bending region in the original material triangulation, not a blend applied to a final solved surface. Its limited strain represents where real finite-stiffness film accommodates a crease transition. The strip material rest metric remains its existing direct developed metric. Shared seam vertices must be single variables/equations, never two meshes glued by world-position blending.

Use one deterministic outward-opening initial fan and a bounded constrained solve: no more than 20 trust-region steps, no more than eight step reductions per step, with an explicit wall-clock cutoff. Seam position/tangent residuals and nonpenetration are hard feasibility gates. Attraction toward approved support and a small dihedral penalty select among feasible poses; neither may trade away feasibility. Failure to find a feasible state within the cap ends this experiment. This proposal deliberately does not prescribe new dependencies or resume an unbounded general optimizer.

At the outer collar/strip join require position equality and matching tangent planes; keep that collar boundary and its first tangent direction tied to the strip. At the inner fan/collar join allow a named physical crease with continuous position. This makes the normal discontinuity a visible fold, not an unnoticed ordinary seam defect. The existing contract calls for tangent continuity at ordinary joins. If the collar cannot meet its metric bound while satisfying these joins, the family fails; do not widen it or increase allowable strain automatically. Refinement uses four three-unit collar rows for the one confirmation run, measuring the same material strain and visual fold footprint.

## Measurement and stop rules

The original whole material rectangle must remain a connected indexed sheet. Account for every triangle, including transparent material topology. Every nondegenerate rigid panel must have both UV-to-world singular values equal to one to numerical precision; collar values must satisfy the declared comparison envelope and strip values retain their independent tolerances. Report shared-seam residuals and the exact worst coordinates. Refining each rigid panel must preserve its metric and embedding; replacing the fan with a finer smooth interpolation is prohibited. The limited compliant collar is intentional: the user requires believable vinyl and legible print, not mathematical inextensibility everywhere.

Unit panel strain is an invariant of this restricted fan construction, not a product requirement. Endpoint displacement divided by collar width is only necessary screening; passing it does not prove 3D collar compatibility. Evaluate both singular values on the complete connected triangulation before advancing to the expensive contact search.

Check every sheet triangle against the actual outer shell and all nonadjacent sheet triangles. Exclude only the common topological vertex/edge itself from selfcontact, not the entire pair: neighboring triangles folded through one another must still fail. Initial accepted geometry must have known exterior connectivity. Report minimum nonadjacent separation; zero-thickness overlap is not an acceptable hidden pleat. Use the existing render contact offset or an explicitly recorded thickness assumption; do not invent a real vinyl thickness claim. A proposed overlapping tuck requires a later finite-thickness contact model and is outside this first outward-opening fan.

Reuse the existing exact-distance adaptive full-triangle certificate, reporting gap by distance from the crease. Do not retain two units as a hard physical premise for this experiment. Measure the smallest feasible maximum lift, the sheet area above two units, its connected footprint and its distance to the actual corner. Straight supported regions outside the declared fan retain the existing bound. A broad tent over the back or front fails localization even with perfect print. Show rear, oblique, side and front views using the original C03 print. Report any physical print occlusion by a fold separately from glyph strain; the original art is retained but a fold can hide it from a particular view.

Stop immediately on an incompatible fan/strip seam, invalid root anchoring, shell penetration, selfintersection, unaccounted material, widespread delamination, or budget exhaustion. A failed fan rejects this kinematic family only. It does not justify cutting artwork, reducing size, rejecting corner centers, or declaring all possible wrinkle patterns impossible.

## Runtime path if the static family passes

Retain the shell collider once per shell identity and only current/previous fan/collar states. Forward hinge transforms cost O(panel count). Keep the fan's material outlet/topology stable over a gesture; recomputing nearest-boundary outlets per pointer move would cause a visible crease jump. Measure the full constrained update including the collar, collision and upload; a small local model can still be too expensive. Provisional engineering gate: p95 completed pose update below one 60Hz frame, with CPU work leaving rendering headroom, on the target mobile viewport/device. A worker alone does not establish this gate.

All geometry, hit testing and contour buffers publish one matching generation. Superseded work cancels, and no invalid intermediate fan appears. Do not hide multi-frame lag by spring-following the pointer. Topology changes, peel/return collision and 45/137-degree cases remain separate gates after this single pose, not claims made by this advisory.

## What would actually require an owner material choice

No new owner choice is justified merely by failed numerical methods: the request already permits realistic local wrinkles. If a complete, reviewable candidate requires conspicuous local delamination or print occlusion, show that exact result and its measured footprint. The decision would concern visible folded vinyl versus a more stretchable smooth film, whose print necessarily changes shape. We do not yet have that evidence, so there is no basis to ask the owner to choose now or to describe a feasibility impossibility.
