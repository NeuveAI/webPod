## Proposed continuous chart

A nearest-edge-normal wrap is rejected: long corner overflow makes neighboring normals converge on the front and creates inverted triangles. Instead use a device-centered radial chart. For each polar direction in modelXY, obtain its actual rear-cap boundary and the ordered external cross-section through rear roll, material seam, front bevel and front surface. Parameterize that meridian by cumulative physical arc length. A material point beyond the rear-cap boundary consumes arc length around this path; its remaining front distance continues radially inward on the actual front. The direction remains device-centered, so corner rays do not focus at the local26-unit corner center.

The center itself must not slide inward when dropped on a shoulder. Invert the actual rear hit into its chart coordinate first, then add rotated artwork offsets there. Flat-cap chart coordinates are unchanged, preserving legacy positions. Apply the local chart metric to offsets at a shoulder center so a unit near the center remains a physical unit rather than an unexplained tangential scale jump. The surface is doubly curved; a single planar vinyl print cannot be perfectly isometric everywhere. Measure bounded stretch/compression and triangle orientation rather than claiming an inextensible corner mapping. Do not add cuts or remove overflow.

Use actual production geometry/parameters for the meridian, not an invented rounded box. Rear sections are explicitly available; front continuation must sample its actual deformed mesh and covering glass/wheel surfaces. Existing fixed rear-only function input is insufficient: a shared wrap-surface sampler must be constructed from actual device surface resources and borrowed by rear print, preview and peel. Device.tsx/StickerSurface and scene integration need explicit owner handoff. The sampler is cached per geometry/form identity and disposed with those resources; material gesture updates do not rebuild shell topology.

## Bounded corner candidate after Cartesian strip validation

### Front residual correction after rejected final-matrix capture

The exact planar inverse Q is required on the flat rear, but its nonlinear residual must not become extra front travel. Use its root Taylor chart L and solve the scalar corrected depth D = origin + L.depth + w(D)(Q.depth−L.depth), then arc=L.arc+w(D)(Q.arc−L.arc). The phase weight is1 at/before the rear cap and0 at/after the actual front rim, with cubic smoothstep between. Q−L and its derivative vanish at the root; the exact root and tangent registration remain. The convex rear boundary yields a nonnegative depth residual, so the scalar equation is monotone; Newton is safeguarded by its endpoint bracket and capped at12 evaluations. This is a chart-coordinate correction fed through the existing supported mapper, not interpolation between world positions.

An explicit phase evaluated at Q.depth is rejected: it folds rows while removing large residuals. The implicit candidate passes bounded full-grid finite/area/normal checks and rear identity, but requires new actual rear/side/front visual evidence. ### Superseding detached interaction contract: frozen material frame

The original pointer coordinate is the zero of this frame, so an offset inside the printed triangle cannot shift the saved center.
