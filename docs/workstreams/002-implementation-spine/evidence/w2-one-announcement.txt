W2.4 / U13 — a 30-detent flick produces exactly ONE announcement
================================================================

001 §4.4 and 001 §15.0 U13: the detent settle is debounced to a single
`aria-live="polite"` summary 350ms after motion stops, and there is no
live-region spam during fast scroll. The failure this prevents is invisible to
anyone not listening to it.

The headline test drives 30 real detents through the real reducer and the real
store — no fake timers, no mocks. Only the clock is supplied, because a
debounce that cannot be told the time cannot be tested at all.
